## ✨ What This App Does

This app instantly creates a **5-day Bible playtime plan** customized for young children.

Each day includes:
- 📖 A short devotional  
- 🎨 Easy crafts and hands-on activities  
- 🎶 Praise prompts or songs  
- 💬 Parent encouragement  
- 📅 A suggested calendar reminder  

Perfect for busy families wanting meaningful, faith-filled moments without complicated prep.

- Christian parents  
- Children ages **2–7**  
- Sunday school helpers  
- Families wanting to plant seeds of faith 🌱  

Warm, simple, joyful, and family-friendly.

---

## 🎁 How It Works

1. Choose a theme (Christmas, Easter, Thankfulness, Joy, Courage, etc.)  
2. Select the children’s age range  
3. Pick a daily duration  
4. Click **Generate**  
5. Receive a personalized, child-friendly 5-day Bible series  

Powered by OpenAI's agentic tools for structured and consistent output.
